University Attendance Policy

Regulation

Mandatory Examination Threshold: As per University Regulations, every student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course to be eligible to appear for semester-end theory and practical examinations.

  • ≥ 75%: Satisfactory attendance • Eligible for exams
  • 70% – 74.9%: Warning status • Medical / Dean clearance required
  • < 70%: Defaulter list • Debarred from university examination

If your attendance is incorrectly recorded, raise an inquiry with your respective subject faculty via the Academic Queries section.
